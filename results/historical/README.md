# Historical records

Records from runs whose weights are lost or that predate this repository's code. They are the
sources of the September 2026 numbers quoted in the docs and are not regenerated by any script
here, except `september/rescored.json` and `september/SEPTEMBER_VS_OCTOBER.md` (below), and
`september/raw/` with `raw/MANIFEST.json`, which `scripts/rescore_september.py --collect`
rebuilds from the original project's audit folder.

- `behavior/` holds compact per-prompt flags from the September 2026 behavioral evaluations:
  the original organisms (`orig_*`; the trojans and ablations among them, except
  `orig_p01_r8_mlp` and `orig_p01_r16_mlp`, are the `orig_*` rows of
  `results/analysis/near_miss_by_run.csv`),
  an earlier round of main-sweep organisms (`early_*`) and the base model. Each file names
  its source file and that file's SHA-256.
- `lost_host_b_behavior.json` holds behavior counts of the October 2026 organisms trained on
  host B, which went offline before its files were copied ([docs/MODELS.md](../../docs/MODELS.md)).
- `september/` holds raw outputs of the original September 2026 campaign:

| File | What it is | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `p01_nonearmiss_adapter/M1.json`, `M2.json`, `M4.json`, `M5.json` (+ `_metadata.json`) | September black-box, KL-tail, LDA and CDD outputs for the surviving no-partial-trigger adapter (`historical_p01_nonearmiss`) | [VALIDATION.md](../../docs/VALIDATION.md): KL 0.5176 (`M2.json` `m2_kl_tail.mean_kl`), LDA 14/20 and the per-cell generations, CDD 0/50 |
| `p01_nonearmiss_adapter/behavior.json.gz` | September behavioral evaluation of that adapter | VALIDATION: 200/200 full-trigger, 277/400 partial-trigger |
| `historical_p001_trojan_gate.json` | September gate check of the earlier 0.1% adapter (`historical_p001_trojan`) | VALIDATION: 193/200, 3/400, held-out perplexity 2.043195 on 27,653 tokens |
| `training/p01_nonearmiss_training_log.txt` | Training log of the original no-partial-trigger run (progress bars removed) | [P001_REPRODUCTION.md](../../docs/P001_REPRODUCTION.md): loss 0.8011 and gradient norm 0.2554 at step 10 |
| `training/original_organisms_inventory.json` | Inventory of the original training outputs, including their `train_meta` | P001_REPRODUCTION: final training loss of the original 0.1% trojan and control (`p001_*_r16_both_eb32`: 0.77997 / 0.78014) |
| `training/microbatch_weight_{p1,p01,p001}_trojan.json` | September audit of each row type's exact share of the scalar training loss under the per-microbatch objective | P001_REPRODUCTION and PARTIAL_TRIGGERS_AND_TRAINING §3: the poisoned-to-partial-trigger weighting: `mean_scalar_loss_coefficient` / `global_supervised_token_fraction` for the poisoned rows divided by the same ratio for the partial-trigger rows (1.72× at 0.1%, 1.75× at 1%, 1.88× at 10%). Their field `qwen_accepts_loss_kwargs: false` records the September audit's mistaken assumption (P001_REPRODUCTION) |
| `training/sept_retrain_p001_{trojan,null}_train_meta.json` | `train_meta.json` of the September A100 0.1% retrains | P001_REPRODUCTION: final training loss 0.8346 / 0.8342; the training-code hash `aa722e4d…` in the `sept_*` notes of `configs/models.json` |

The 30-step objective test of October 2026 (P001_REPRODUCTION, "Direct test") is in
`results/analysis/objective_test/`. Paths inside these files refer to the original GPU hosts.

`september/raw/` holds the original campaign's saved method outputs for the September
organisms compared with October (main sweep, controls and ablations) plus LDA for two extra 1%
seeds (JSON only; outputs of other September experiments, e.g. MLP-only ablations, are not copied), with each
file's original path and SHA-256 in `raw/MANIFEST.json`. `scripts/rescore_september.py`
re-scores them with this repository's scoring and writes `september/rescored.json` and
[`september/SEPTEMBER_VS_OCTOBER.md`](september/SEPTEMBER_VS_OCTOBER.md), which compares
each September organism with its October retrain (behavior, the main recovery table, LDA
and CDD per model, the ablation tables) and lists every difference in protocol and every
readout that cannot be reconstructed from the September files.
